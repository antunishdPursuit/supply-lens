Where our data comes from
Tradeverifyd
Company records, trade relationships with product codes, ownership links and stored government listings. Pulled on 19 November 2026 through Tradeverifyd's read-only data tools and cached.
Sayari
Ownership percentages, subsidiaries, shipment counts and risk factors. Read from Sayari Graph on 19 November 2026 with event access and Sayari's permission, and copied into our cached files.
Tavily
Web search. All web evidence was retrieved on 19 November 2026 at about 12:20 PM ET and saved; the site does not search live. Tavily did not return publication dates for these results, so each source shows its retrieval time; government listings show their listing dates.
Maps
Country shapes from Natural Earth via the world-atlas package.
Method and limits
  • Listings are records stored by the data providers, not a live sanctions screening.
  • A trade or ownership link shows a connection. It is not proof that a specific product involved forced labour.
  • We found and corrected data errors: a different company (Beijing Tsingke Biotech) merged into Chenguang's record, an entity named "Canada" in a trade route, and McCormick recorded as "Banrion, LLC".
  • "Alerts" means listings stored in a database. "Risk factors" are Sayari's labels, including risk passed down through ownership. The counts measure different things.
  • Chinese subsidiary names were translated by us. Some map positions are approximate (city or country level).
